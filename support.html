Questions

How do I learn to play?
Open How to Play from the opening screen or the game menu. In your first turns, the Getting Started checklist at the top of the map shows the next step.
Is my campaign saved?
Yes, automatically at every turn. Choose Continue on the opening screen, or Load Game for older saves. Saves stay on your device.
Why is there no music?
Music starts switched off. Tap the music button at the top right of the opening screen, or raise Music in Settings.
Battles take too long.
Turn on Quick battles in Settings: each battle is then decided at once and you see the result.
The game is too easy, or too hard.
Choose the rivals' strength (Easy, Normal or Hard) when you choose your realm. Each realm also has its own difficulty, shown by its stars.
Can I play on a computer?
Yes: play the web version in your browser. Saves there are kept by the browser.
