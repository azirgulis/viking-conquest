Privacy Policy

Britannia 793: Grand Strategy (formerly Viking Conquest). Last updated October 27, 2026.

Britannia 793 is a strategy game made by Aras Zirgulis. It collects no personal data. This page explains what that means for the iPhone and iPad app and for the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party services that collect data. We never see who plays or how.

Your saved games and settings

Your saved campaigns and your settings (sound levels, quick battles, the Getting Started checklist) are stored only on your device, or in your browser's storage when you play the web version. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

The internet

The iPhone and iPad app does not need an internet connection and sends nothing over the network. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages: when your browser loads the game, GitHub receives the usual request information, such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself sends nothing once it has loaded.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
